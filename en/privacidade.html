Data controller

The data controller is Ricardo Duarte, a sole trader providing computer repairs under the trading name Biochip - Infordomicilio. The service operates at customers’ premises, with no shop open to the public.

For personal data enquiries: geral.biochip@gmail.com · +351 926 014 310.

Data and purposes

We collect name, email, location, requested service and message to reply, prepare quotes and arrange assistance. Service orders may record name, phone, email, equipment, fault and work performed. Do not send passwords, bank details or unnecessary confidential information through the form.

Legal basis for processing

Processing required to prepare a requested quote or deliver assistance is based on pre-contractual steps and contract performance under GDPR Article 6(1)(b). Retention required by law relies on Article 6(1)(c). Any retention to defend rights requires an individual assessment of legitimate interest and necessity.

Acknowledging that you have read the policy is not consent to marketing. Contacts are not used for advertising.

Where enquiries are processed

The form is processed by Formspree and forwarded to geral.biochip@gmail.com. Information needed for the service order is recorded in GestOS, installed locally on the controller’s computer. According to the controller’s stated arrangements, only Ricardo Duarte has access to enquiries and GestOS.

Technical providers may process data to deliver their services under their terms. Exclusive access refers to the internal management of the business and does not exclude technical processing by providers.

Retention and deletion

  • Enquiries not pursued: deleted from GestOS and Gmail as soon as it is confirmed they will not proceed.
  • Completed services: service orders are kept for the warranty period applicable to the work.
  • Justified exceptions: documents required by law, or data strictly necessary for a dispute or defence of rights, follow applicable retention periods and legal bases. They are not deleted simply because the warranty expires.

Deleting enquiries is a procedure performed by the controller, not an automatic website function. Deletion within the business does not itself delete technical records retained by providers under their terms.

Providers and external communications

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Formspree processes submissions and Gmail receives enquiries. If you choose WhatsApp, the message is transmitted through that channel when sent. The OpenStreetMap map loads only after a click and involves communication between your browser and that provider.

Formspree’s submission storage and technical retention terms are still being checked. We do not claim that it stores no data. Consult the policies of Formspree, Google and GitHub.

Processing outside the European Economic Area may occur, depending on the provider and configuration. The controller must verify contractual terms and applicable transfer safeguards. We do not claim that all data is processed exclusively in Portugal or the European Union.

Data subject rights

You may request access, correction, erasure, restriction and portability where applicable at geral.biochip@gmail.com. Where processing relies on legitimate interest, you may object under the law. Proportionate identity verification may be required.

You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Portuguese Data Protection Authority (CNPD). This form does not implement automated decision-making or profiling of enquiries.

Required fields and alternative channels

Required fields are necessary to assess and reply through the form. If you do not provide them, you can contact us by phone or email. Sending a request does not confirm an appointment or acceptance of a quote.

Information still to be completed by the controller

Provider storage terms, contracts and transfers remain to be checked. Identification and legally required business contact details, including the applicable contact address, must be confirmed separately; having no shop does not replace that verification.
