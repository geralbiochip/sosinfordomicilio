No travel needed

We carry out many of our services remotely

In many cases you do not need to travel or wait for a visit: the work can be done remotely on your computer, with your authorisation. For example:

  • Software installation.
  • General settings.
  • Setting up email accounts.
  • Antivirus installation.
  • Microsoft Office installation.

Remote access is done with your consent and does not replace physical work where needed. Licences and subscriptions, where they exist, are clarified before we proceed.

In detail

What we can do remotely

Software installation

We install and set up the programs you need, after confirming compatibility with your equipment.

General settings

System and program adjustments so the computer works the way you expect, as agreed.

Email accounts

Setting up email accounts on your computer so you can read them without hassle.

Antivirus installation

Installing and setting up a protection solution suited to your equipment.

Microsoft Office

Installing Microsoft Office and help with the initial setup, subject to the applicable licence or subscription.

Usage questions

Clarifying problems using programs and the system, when they can be handled remotely.

Before the visit

What you need to have

Describe the problem and confirm the computer switches on and has Internet access. Close personal documents not needed for the session and keep the login details of the accounts we will set up close by.

Quote and authorisation

The scope, licences and applicable costs are explained before we proceed. The session only begins with your authorisation.

Limits and important precautions

Not every problem can be solved remotely. Physical faults or being unable to connect may require an on-site visit. We do not bypass passwords or security mechanisms.

From Cascais to Lisbon

On-site assistance by appointment along the Cascais Line, in Sintra, Oeiras and Lisbon. The business operates as a sole trader, with no shop open to the public.

Step by step

How the service works

01 · Describe your request

Tell us what you need and identify the equipment. Do not send passwords in the form.

02 · We book the session

We agree the day, time and connection tool. We explain the procedure and how to end the connection.

03 · We do the work

With your authorisation, we handle the request remotely and you can follow what is being done.

Frequently asked questions

Can I follow the remote session?

Yes. The session is agreed with you and you can follow what is done. Authorisation and control options depend on the tool used.

Do I need to install anything?

It depends on the connection tool. We explain before the session what is needed and how to end access.

Which problems cannot be solved remotely?

Physical faults, equipment that will not switch on or has no Internet access may need an on-site visit or another solution.

Do you need remote help?

Explain what you need and we will arrange the session.
