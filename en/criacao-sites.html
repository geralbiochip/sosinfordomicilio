Benefits

Benefits of having a website, even if you already have social media

Having a website and being on social media are complementary strategies. Social media help you promote and talk to people; the website gathers your business information in a space you control.

A digital asset of your own

With your own domain and content, you do not depend on a platform’s rules, policy changes or an account being blocked.

Less risk of losing your history

If a social media account is compromised or deleted, posts and contacts can be lost. The website keeps the essential information in a place of your own.

Control over design and features

You decide the structure, the look and what visitors can do, without being limited to each network’s format.

An experience tailored to your brand

A design of your own presents the business, the services and the way you work better.

More credibility

A professional website helps people who do not know you yet understand who you are and how to ask for information.

Custom features

Forms, blog, frequently asked questions, online shop or other tools, depending on the project’s needs.

Visitor data under your control

Where applicable and in compliance with the law, you can collect enquiries and analyse visits in a more controlled way. Personal data requires proper information and consent where necessary.

Ready for search

With well-organised structure and content, the website can be found in search engines. There is no guarantee of rankings or visits.

Connection with social media

The website can receive visits from social media and show links or content from your profiles, strengthening promotion.

Room for detailed information

Services, terms, documentation and frequently asked questions can be explained in more detail than in a post.

Accessible to more people

People who do not use a particular network can view the website without creating an account.

A more stable presence in the long term

Social networks change, lose reach or become restricted in some regions. A website can be maintained and updated over the years.

The ideal is to combine both channels: social media bring you closer to people and the website shows clearly what you do and how to contact you.

After launch

Hosting and maintenance, with clear terms.

We offer a plan with hosting, SSL certificate and technical maintenance on a monthly subscription, according to the proposal. Domain registration or renewal, content changes and other services must be expressly identified.

Before proceeding, we clarify who manages the domain, which access is handed over, how support works and the conditions for renewal, cancellation and any transfer.

The plan should identify

  • Hosting, capacity and resources contracted.
  • Domain, ownership, renewal and related costs.
  • SSL certificate and its management.
  • Applicable updates to the technology used.
  • Backups: frequency, retention and restoration.
  • Content changes included and usage limits.
  • Agreed channels, hours and response times.
  • Licences, external services and recurring costs.

Having backups or maintenance is not a guarantee against faults or of recovery in any circumstances.

Before you proceed

Frequently asked questions about website creation

How long does the project take?

The timeline is set in the proposal, after assessing the scope. Delivery of text, images, access and approvals affects deadlines. Additional features may require a revised timeline.

Do I have to provide the text and photographs?

We can work with your materials or plan support in preparing the content. It is agreed who writes, reviews and approves each element. Images must have suitable usage rights; stock images and photography may carry extra costs.

Can I update the website myself?

It depends on the technical solution chosen. If you need to edit content, say so at the start so we can assess an admin area and the training needed. On a site without an editor, changes can be made through the support you contract.

Will the website appear on Google?

We can prepare the technical structure and agreed elements to make the content easier to discover and understand. Indexing and ranking depend on search engines and several factors; we do not guarantee positions, traffic or sales.

Will the domain and access be in my name?

Domain ownership, access and rights over deliverables must be set out in the proposal. Licences, platforms and third-party components keep their providers’ terms. Delivery and transfer are agreed before contracting.

Do you handle privacy and cookies?

We can implement the texts supplied or approved by the person responsible for the site and the agreed technical mechanisms. Forms, statistics, maps and other external services are assessed in the project. We do not replace legal advice nor promise automatic legal compliance.

Is there maintenance after publication?

A subscription with hosting, SSL and technical maintenance can be contracted, with a defined scope. Changes, response times, backups and excluded services are specified. We do not assume permanent support or unlimited changes.

Can I have a website in several languages?

Yes, once the pages and languages are defined in the proposal. Writing, translating, reviewing and updating each version needs identified owners and may carry extra costs.

Can you reuse my current website?

We assess the platform, content, access and terms of use. It may be possible to renew, migrate or rebuild the site. Redirects and preserving addresses are analysed where applicable.

Do you have examples of your work?

Ask for references in your first contact. Any example shared should state whether it is a published work, a concept or a mock-up. We do not present demonstrations as client projects.

Shall we shape your next website?

Tell us what you do and what you would like to improve in your online presence.
