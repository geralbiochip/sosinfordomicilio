Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions about travel, diagnosis and appointments.

Is there a travel charge?

No. Travel within the service areas shown on the website is free, by appointment. Outside these areas, use the form so we can assess your request.

Is diagnosis charged?

Equipment diagnosis for repair is free. The specific Remote Technical Diagnosis session, including network assessment and equipment model recommendations, costs €50. Repairs, parts and other services are quoted and only proceed with approval.

Do you provide on-site IT support?

Yes. I visit homes and offices in Greater Lisbon and the Cascais area to solve computer, printer and Wi-Fi problems and install or configure equipment. Outside the on-site service area, we can arrange a remote session if the issue can be handled remotely.

Do I need to make a backup?

A backup is not required to request assistance. Tell us if you have one; otherwise, let us know about important files on the device. We agree precautions for your data before working on it. We cannot guarantee data preservation or recovery in every case.

When can a problem be resolved remotely?

Remote support is by appointment. Describe the issue in the form so we can assess whether it can be handled remotely. If suitable, we agree the date, time and session terms with your authorisation.

What if the equipment cannot be repaired?

If repair is impossible or not worthwhile, we explain the diagnosis and discuss available alternatives. We can assess file recovery options or help you choose replacement equipment, without guaranteeing these options are possible in every case. Any additional service requires your approval.

Do you repair computers?

Yes. I diagnose and repair desktops and laptops: slow performance, startup failures, replacing drives with SSDs, cleaning, and memory or component upgrades. I assess the equipment and provide a quote for approval before repairing it.

How quickly do you respond?

For urgent requests, call or use the WhatsApp button on the home page: I aim to reply the same day, subject to availability. I reply to form and email enquiries within 24 business hours. Response time is not necessarily the time until an intervention.

What does the €50 Remote Technical Diagnosis include?

A remote session to assess the existing network and equipment, investigate the cause of the issue and provide concrete recommendations. If equipment needs to be purchased, you receive a list of recommended models. You are free to use these recommendations without hiring me for installation.

Appointments and proposed services are confirmed individually. No repair starts without approval.
