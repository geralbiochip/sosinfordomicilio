IT security and backups

Practical measures to reduce risks, assess suspicious behaviour and prepare copies of important data.

Request an assessmentView all services

Service tailored to your request

How we can help

Protecting a computer depends on several precautions, not a promise of total security. The service starts from how you use the equipment, the data to preserve and the signs observed to define a proportionate response.

We help identify unwanted programs and set up routines to protect your files, adapted to how the equipment is used.

  • Analysis and removal of viruses, malware and unwanted extensions.
  • Setting up protection tools and security updates.
  • Checking the security options of the system and browser.
  • Setting up backups to external drives or cloud services chosen by the customer.
  • Defining folders to protect, frequency and file recovery tests.
  • Guidance on fraudulent messages, passwords and two-factor authentication.

No solution guarantees absolute protection or recovery of all files. Backups should be checked regularly.

In detail

What we can assess and prepare

Assessing suspicious signs

We assess alerts, unknown applications and changes in behaviour reported in your request. Where appropriate, we carry out checks and explain the limits of the conclusions possible.

System protection

We can review protection and update options compatible with the equipment. Setup is agreed with the owner and does not replace safe habits or protecting accounts with their respective providers.

Backup planning

We help identify priority files, where copies go and how often. External drives, online space and their subscriptions are assessed according to volume and purpose.

Checking and restoring

When contracted, the work can include setup and restore tests on a sample of data. Frequency, retention, responsibilities and follow-up must be defined in the service.

Before the visit

What we need to know

Describe the alerts and identify the data you cannot lose. Say whether copies already exist and where they are kept. Do not send sensitive files, unnecessary personal documents or credentials through the form.

Quote and authorisation

The scope, parts, licences and applicable costs are explained before we proceed. Travel, availability and service conditions are confirmed based on your request. Additional work requires assessment and approval.

Limits and important precautions

We do not guarantee the absence of threats, invulnerability or recovery of all files. Complex incident response and forensic analysis are not included by default. Any tool installation or restoration is agreed in advance.

Frequently asked questions

Does antivirus software guarantee I am protected?

We do not present any tool as an absolute guarantee. The assessment also considers settings, updates and how the equipment is used.

Are backups made automatically?

They can be set up that way when the solution and access allow it. Frequency, space and checking need to be defined.

Does the price include drives or online space?

Only if it appears in the quote. Equipment, licences and subscriptions are itemised separately where applicable.

Shall we assess your request?

Explain the problem and we will agree the next step.
